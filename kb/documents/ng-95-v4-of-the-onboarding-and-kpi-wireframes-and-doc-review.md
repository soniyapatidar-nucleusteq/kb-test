# NG-95: v4 of the onboarding and KPI wireframes and doc: review feedback and achievable KPIs

Source: NG-95

v4 of the onboarding and KPI wireframes and doc: review feedback and achievable KPIs

Type: Subtask
Status: Done

Updated Harsh's v3 Personas and Dashboards doc and the onboarding and KPI wireframes to v4, using the weekly review feedback.
Scope: dashboards cover real-time system health only. Ticket tiles are parked, and ticket settings are removed from onboarding.

Blast radius: kept, and widened to a full-width view showing how many kinds of signal agree on each affected node.

Onboarding: captures organisation details, environments, member permissions with proposed defaults, and one monitored branch per repository (the one that deploys to production). Staging is dropped.

KPI trim: dashboards show 25 of the 53 KPIs, the ones that matter most and that today's data, or the v2 tables, can fill. The other 28 are listed as "Later", each with its reason.

Left out: the model limitations (z-score, LSTM). They are covered by the separate approach document.
